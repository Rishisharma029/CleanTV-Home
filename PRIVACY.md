# CleanTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

CleanTV ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy and transparently handling your device information.

## 1. Information We Collect
CleanTV is an on-device optimization, system management, and lean home launcher companion for Android TV and Windows desktop devices.

- **Local Device Operations**: CleanTV performs all ADB communication, package inspections, bloatware debloating, and memory management locally between your personal computer and your Android TV device.
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII)**: We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data such as your name, contact information, photos, files, or sensitive credentials.
- **License Verification**: When activating a CleanTV Pro license, the cryptographic key format is verified algorithmically on-device or against Microsoft Store receipt tokens. No telemetry or tracking profile is built.

## 2. Permissions & Security
- **ADB (Android Debug Bridge)**: Used strictly to query installed packages, manage app states, and send remote control key events as requested by the user.
- **Local Backups**: Any debloat backups created by CleanTV are saved solely to your local computer (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\CleanTV\backups`) and are never uploaded to any remote cloud.

## 3. Third-Party Services
If purchasing through the Microsoft Store, transactions are governed by Microsoft's standard commercial terms and Privacy Statement. We do not store or process your financial payment cards directly.
